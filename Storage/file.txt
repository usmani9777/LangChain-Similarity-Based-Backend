COMPANY POLICY MANUAL
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: January 2026


1. INTRODUCTION

This document outlines the official policies and procedures of AcmeTech Solutions.
All employees, contractors, and affiliates are required to read, understand, and
comply with the policies described in this manual.

The purpose of these policies is to ensure a safe, productive, ethical, and legally
compliant workplace environment. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action,
including termination of employment.


2. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to behave professionally at all times. Respect, integrity,
and accountability form the foundation of our workplace culture.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, misuse of company
resources, or any conduct that may harm the company’s reputation. Employees must
report any violations immediately to Human Resources.


3. WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE

Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees are expected to be punctual and adhere to their assigned schedules.

Any absence, delay, or early departure must be communicated to the reporting
manager in advance. Repeated attendance issues may lead to corrective action.


4. DATA PRIVACY & INFORMATION SECURITY

The company is committed to protecting confidential and sensitive information.
Employees must not share internal data with unauthorized individuals or external
entities.

Passwords must be kept secure, and company systems should only be accessed for
authorized business purposes. Any suspected data breach must be reported
immediately to the IT department.


5. REMOTE WORK POLICY

Remote work is permitted subject to managerial approval. Employees working remotely
must maintain productivity levels equivalent to on-site work.

Company data accessed remotely must follow the same security standards. Public Wi-Fi
networks should be avoided unless a secure VPN is used.


6. USE OF COMPANY ASSETS

Company assets including laptops, software licenses, and office equipment are
provided strictly for business use.

Employees are responsible for the proper care of assigned assets. Any damage or loss
must be reported promptly.


7. HEALTH & SAFETY

The company strives to maintain a safe working environment. Employees must comply
with all safety regulations and report hazardous conditions immediately.

Emergency exits, fire equipment, and first-aid kits must not be obstructed or misused.


8. TERMINATION & EXIT PROCEDURES

Upon termination or resignation, employees must return all company property.
Access to company systems will be revoked on the last working day.

Exit interviews may be conducted to gather feedback and ensure a smooth transition.


9. POLICY UPDATES

The company reserves the right to modify these policies at any time. Employees will be
notified of significant changes through official communication channels.

Continued employment implies acceptance of updated policies.


END OF DOCUMENT
