Donald J. Trump is the 47th and current President of the United States, having assumed office for his second term on January 20, 2025. He previously served as the 45th president from 2017 to 2021. Trump is the second president in U.S. history to serve non-consecutive terms, the first being Grover Cleveland.

Presidency (2025–Present): Entering the second year of his second term in January 2026, Trump's administration has focused on aggressive domestic and foreign policy shifts.

Legislative Action: Signed the "One Big Beautiful Bill Act", a reconciliation bill addressing tax policy, border security, immigration, and energy production.

Foreign Policy: His second term has been marked by activity involving Venezuela and Iran. He met with Venezuelan opposition leader Maria Corina Machado.

Health Policy: Unveiled a new healthcare plan aimed at lowering costs. He also updated the childhood vaccination schedule.

Greenland Crisis: Renewed interest in purchasing Greenland and appointed a special envoy to negotiate with Denmark.

Personal and Business Background:
Born June 14, 1946, in Queens, New York. Graduated from the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School of Finance in 1968.

Before politics, he was a real estate developer and media personality, leading the Trump Organization and hosting "The Apprentice".

Family: Married to Melania Trump since 2005. Children include Donald Jr., Ivanka, Eric, Tiffany, and Barron.

Legal and Political History:
Impeached twice by the House of Representatives in 2019 and 2021 and acquitted both times by the Senate.

In May 2024, he was convicted on 34 felony counts of falsifying business records in New York. Other cases were dismissed following the 2024 election.
